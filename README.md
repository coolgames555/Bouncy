# Bouncy Wall Castle Defense
This game was a project that we spent roughly 10 hours on for the Hack Club Haven game jam. This game is about protecting your castle from the oncoming catapult ammo by placing walls which will make them "Bounce Back".
## Controls
To start, click endless mode, and once you are in the game you must place the walls on the lawn of the castle to deflect the ammo. to reload your wall, simply click on the wall at the bottom of the screen and place that new wall. Be weary however, because your walls are not permanent.
